I explored and learned several important Excel tools and techniques that helped me transform raw sales data into meaningful insights and a visually appealing dashboard.

Here’s what I’ve learned and applied:
 ✅ XLOOKUP & VLOOKUP for data lookup
 ✅ Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts for analysis
 ✅ Slicers for easy data filtering
 ✅ Creating dynamic and interactive dashboards
 ✅ Using colors and formatting to make visuals more engaging and effective

🔍 Key Insights from the Project:

📅 Busiest Days & Times:
Orders are highest on weekends — especially Friday and Saturday evenings.
The busiest hours are 12–1 PM and 5–8 PM, when most pizza orders come in.

🍕 Best & Worst Sellers:
Best Sellers: Classic Deluxe & Chicken pizzas are top performers and major revenue generators.
Worst Seller: The Brie Carre pizza ranks lowest in both orders and revenue.

📊 Category & Size Performance:
The Classic Category contributes to the maximum sales and total orders.
Large-size pizzas generate the highest sales overall.
